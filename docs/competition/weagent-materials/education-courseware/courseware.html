Wise or Foolish?

叙事阅读与写作 · A Storybook Lesson

Today we open a real story and ask one question: was the choice wise or foolish? You will read, judge, and write an analysis paragraph. 今天，我们一起翻开一个真实的故事，判断主人公的选择是明智还是愚蠢，并写出一段有理有据的分析。

本课任务：读懂故事 → 做出判断 → 写出分析段落（Read → Judge → Write）

The Story Opens…

story

Once upon a time — in a very real life — someone stood at a fork in the road. Step into their shoes. 很久以前，就在很真实的生活里，有人站在了岔路口。先设想你站在他的位置。

A big reward comes with a big risk — what would you do? 巨大回报伴随巨大风险，你会怎么做？
  • Every story has a decision point. 每个故事都有抉择时刻。
  • A choice leads to consequences. 选择带来后果。
  • Our job: judge it with evidence. 用证据做判断。
教师备注 Speaker Notes

提问：如果是你，会怎么做？先不给出结论。

The Real Case: Nadia's Choice

Nadia, a 17-year-old swimmer, trained three years for the national championships. The final fell on her grandmother's 80th birthday — the grandmother who raised her. Nadia chose the birthday.

Decision 决定：family over competition. Consequence 后果：missed the nationals.

The Analysis Paragraph: Claim–Evidence–Commentary

framework
A strong analysis paragraph = Claim + Evidence + Commentary. 一个有力的分析段落 = 观点 + 证据 + 阐释。
  • Claim 观点：state your judgement in one clear sentence. 用一句话亮明你的判断。
  • Evidence 证据：give facts or short quotes from the text. 引用文中的事实或短句。
  • Commentary 阐释：explain why the evidence supports your claim. 解释证据为何支持你的观点。
Use linkers to connect ideas: in my view, on the other hand, as a result. 用连接词让逻辑更清晰：在我看来、另一方面、因此。
教师备注 Speaker Notes

讲解 C-E-C 三步结构及连接词用法，给出中英对照示例；强调顺序：先观点、再证据、后阐释。

Page 6

A Model Analysis Paragraph

model

A Model: The Grasshopper's Choice

In my view, the grasshopper's choice was foolish: he sang all summer while the ant stored food, so he starved in winter. Planning ahead matters more than pleasure.
  • Claim 观点：the choice was foolish.
  • Evidence 证据：sang all summer; the ant stored food.
  • Commentary 阐释：pleasure led to hunger.
Structure only — your judgement is yours. 只示范结构，判断由你论证。
教师备注 Speaker Notes

示范只展示结构，不替代学生对 Nadia 的判断；标注 C-E-C 三处。

Page 7

Your Turn: Write Your Analysis

activity
Write one paragraph (80–100 words): Was Nadia's choice wise or foolish? Use Claim–Evidence–Commentary. 写一段 80–100 词的分析。
  • Start with a clear claim. 先亮明观点。
  • Give two pieces of evidence. 给两条证据。
  • Explain why they matter. 解释证据为何重要。
  • Use one linker: in my view / as a result. 至少用一个连接词。
Either judgement is fine — evidence matters most. 两种判断皆可，证据最重要。
教师备注 Speaker Notes

明确字数与时间（约 8 分钟）；巡视并提供支架。

Page 8

Peer Review Checklist

review
Read your partner's paragraph. Does it convince you? 读一读同伴的段落：它说服你了吗？
  • Claim 观点：Is the judgement clear and stated first? 判断是否明确且放在开头？
  • Evidence 证据：Are there at least two pieces of evidence? 是否有至少两条证据？
  • Commentary 阐释：Does the writer explain why the evidence matters? 是否解释了证据的重要性？
  • Language 语言：Are linkers used correctly? 连接词使用是否正确？
Give one strength and one suggestion. 给同伴一个优点和一个改进建议。
教师备注 Speaker Notes

按清单互评：观点是否明确、证据是否充分、阐释是否到位；提醒先肯定优点再提建议。

Page 9

Storybook Ending: What We Learned

closing
Read stories to judge real choices: look for evidence, weigh consequences, then decide. 读故事，学判断：找证据、看后果、下判断。
  • A choice has reasons and consequences. 选择有原因，也有后果。
  • A judgement needs evidence. 判断需要证据。
  • A strong paragraph: Claim + Evidence + Commentary. 有力的段落：观点 + 证据 + 阐释。
The end of one story is the beginning of your own — your analysis, your judgement. 一个故事的结束，是你自己故事的开始。你的分析，由你判断。
教师备注 Speaker Notes

回顾 C-E-C 框架与证据意识；布置课后作业：写一段分析，判断你自己或身边一个真实选择是 wise 还是 foolish，并用证据支撑。
